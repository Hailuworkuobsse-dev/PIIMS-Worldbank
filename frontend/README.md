# PIIMS Frontend

**Project Inception Information Management System** — impact-enabled, bilingual, offline-capable, accessible.

Financed by the World Bank · Owned by the Ministry of Water and Energy, Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia.

---

## What is PIIMS?

A 23-module, 43-role information system covering:

- Project registry and pipeline (M2)
- Inception reports and stage gates (M3)
- Results framework (M4)
- Financial and procurement tracking (M5, M6)
- Supplier portal (M7)
- Beneficiary registry and service delivery (M8)
- GIS mapping (M9)
- Document management (M10)
- Reporting and dashboards (M11, M12)
- Grievance redress (M13)
- Impact measurement suite (M18–M23)
- Administration and audit (M1, M16, M17)

Full specification lives in:

- `SAD/SDD v2.0` — architecture
- `DMP & Data Dictionary v2.0` — data
- `API Spec v2.0` — interfaces
- `UI/UX Spec v2.0` — design
- `SRD v1.0` — requirements
